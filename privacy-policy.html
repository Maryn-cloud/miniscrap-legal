MiniScrap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Thanh Lich Luong · Contact: miniscrap.app@gmail.com

MiniScrap is a journal, planner and habit app. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your pages, habits, check-ins, moods, stickers you place, photos you add to pages and your Home banner are saved only on your device, and are part of your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup). We cannot see them. They are removed when you delete the app, unless you restore your phone from a backup.

You can also save a backup file (Profile → Backup). The file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to put it (Files, iCloud Drive, email…). We never receive it.

2. Data we collect when you use Community (optional)

MiniScrap has no sign-up and never asks for your email. The first time you share or like a template, or report one, the app creates an anonymous Community account for you: a random ID kept on your device. With it we store:

Data Why Visible to others
The display name, handle and bio in your profile Showing who shared a template Yes
Templates you share, including any photos and text in them Showing them in Community Yes
Likes and reports you make Running Community and keeping it safe Likes: as a count. Reports: no
The random account ID Linking the above together No

Follows and the creators you hide are kept only on your device. If you delete the app or change phones, the anonymous account stays behind: your shared templates remain public until you or we remove them, so delete your Community data first (section 6).

Community data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase (servers in Sydney, Australia).

3. Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. To know whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store.

4. Feedback

If you send feedback, the app opens your own email app with a message to us. We receive whatever you choose to send, and use it only to reply and improve the app.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no data brokers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera. Photos are read only when you pick them yourself.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your Community data at any time in the app: Profile → Community data → Delete my Community data. This immediately removes your shared templates and their photos, your likes, your public profile and your Community account from our servers. Reports you filed are kept without any link to you, so moderation records stay intact. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax.
  • If you can no longer reach the app (for example after changing phones), email miniscrap.app@gmail.com with the template names and we will remove them.
  • Content that breaks the Community Guidelines may be removed, and the account may be suspended.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your account data, or ask any question about it, at miniscrap.app@gmail.com.

7. Children

MiniScrap is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has shared something in Community, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, California law or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its use. Write to miniscrap.app@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
